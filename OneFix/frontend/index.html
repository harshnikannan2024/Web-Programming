Operational Trajectory

From concern to closure.

A continuous, documented operational workflow ensuring no verified submission is neglected or buried in bureaucracy.

01

Report

Submitter logs issue description, location, and category. Personal identity attributes are deliberately decoupled at the intake layer.

02

Review

Authorized triage members evaluate urgency, confirm facility location, and categorize the grievance against organizational standards.

03

Assign

The issue is dispatched to the relevant operational lead or maintenance team with assigned responsibility and a logged timestamp.

04

Resolve

Corrective action is executed, verified, and recorded in the audit log. The submitter inspects the resolution note using their private key.

Intended Privacy Architecture

Anonymity is a structural feature, not a setting.

In academic institutions, corporate offices, and industrial plants, people hesitate to report broken equipment, code violations, or safety hazards when their names are permanently attached. Fear of social friction or administrative bias leads to compounding operational failures.

In the intended OneFix workflow, submitter identity is not exposed to organization administrators. Complainants receive an unguessable tracking code to check status independently — no email registration, student profile, or login credentials required.
